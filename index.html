What to include in your report

Where

Station, platform, line, bus route or vehicle number.

Who

A description of anyone involved: clothing, height, direction they headed.

What and when

What happened, what time, and whether anyone is hurt or in danger.

Who responds

CMTP covers all rail and bus. Airport grounds are covered by FLX Police, and Union Station, City Metro events and offices are also supported by Transit Security.

Not sure where to go?

Ask any officer on a platform. Every rail station has at least two officers through the Platform Presence Program. Prefer in person? Find a CMTP location.
